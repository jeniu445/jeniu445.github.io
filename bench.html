Bench

Back

This sculptural representation of a “useless bench” examines how the popularization of modernist design has been used by cities as a justification for creating hostile urban environments. Under the guise of efficiency, cleanliness, and progress, these spaces are increasingly designed to exclude—most visibly targeting unhoused populations, but ultimately affecting the public at large. By prioritizing control over care, modernist urban interventions often introduce physical and psychological barriers that discourage community building and human interaction.
